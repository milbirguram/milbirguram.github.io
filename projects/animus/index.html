Animus

Two light sensors, two wheels and a few wires are enough to make a machine look as if it fears, loves, attacks or explores. Wire one in the workshop and release it among the lights. Then go the other way: work out a hidden wiring from what a vehicle does. After Valentino Braitenberg’s Vehicles (1984). The vehicles have no inertia and never collide, and the floor wraps at its edges; the fear and the love are in the person watching.
