Facade

A short drive through barn country, then ten small cases. In each, look round the scene, pick up what you find, and finish the sentence: your answer, and the evidence you are going on. Every case is scored twice, once for being right and once for knowing. The game counts it as knowing only when your answer was true because of that evidence, not by luck; philosophers dispute that rule in some cases, fake barns among them.

That is a barn — because it looks like one.
