Bromide

Type a sentence and see which of your words were predictable and which were surprising, measured in bits: the less guessable a word, the more information it carries. Then rewrite to raise the number. The guesser is a small language model counted from a few dozen English novels and essays of about 1810 to 1920, texts from Project Gutenberg. It sees only the two words or punctuation marks before each word, so “predictable” here means predictable to those old books, not to you, and not to today.
