Almanac

A small data pipeline meant to run once a day: it fetches the hourly Air Quality Health Index for seven western Canadian cities, checks each day against a written contract, stores it, and reports on itself. The answer comes first, then the pipeline’s own health, run by run. The readings are real-time and unverified, and the record begins on 4 August 2026, so the counts are hours since then, not this year. Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada, under its data licence.

The question

How many hours has the air been high-risk (AQHI 7 or more) in these western Canadian cities?

– hours

The AQHI is ECCC’s 1 to 10+ scale of health risk from air pollution. 7 to 10 is “high risk”; smoke from wildfires is the usual cause in summer. An hour counts when its reading rounds to 7 or more. Days are UTC days.

Contract results

A data contract is a written promise about what each day’s data must look like. Before loading anything, the pipeline checks the day against the seven promises below. A small breach marks the day with a warning and it is loaded with a note; a serious one marks it refused, and the day is not quietly stored. The promises are kept as data in contract.json.
