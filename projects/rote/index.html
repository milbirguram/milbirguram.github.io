Rote

Before a speech synthesiser can say “£900” it must decide on “nine hundred pounds”. Type a sentence, or pull one from held-out test data, and compare four ways of deciding. Then switch off, one at a time, the tricks behind the 99.5% accuracy that conTEXT, my 2018 SFU group project with Siddhant Singhal, Raghib Musarrat, Rahul Rajeev and Greg Mori, reported. “Correct” here means matching Google’s Kestrel system, itself largely hand-written grammars.
