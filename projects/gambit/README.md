# Gambit

## What it is for

Play a small chess engine while seeing its search depth, work, candidates and expected line. The point is to make a tree search and a position formula visible, without claiming a measured playing rating.

## How I know

`model.js` is original, buildless JavaScript shared by Node and a dedicated Web Worker. Legal moves use a 0x88 board; legal filtering handles discovered checks, castling, en passant and all promotions. FEN and SAN are supported. Checkmate takes precedence over draw rules. Repetition keys include en passant only when a legal capture exists. The interface automatically claims threefold and fifty-move draws; it conservatively recognises bare kings, a single bishop/knight and bishops confined to one square colour as insufficient material. It does not implement competition clocks or claim procedures.

The engine uses iterative deepening, negamax alpha–beta, capture ordering (MVV-LVA), killer moves and capture quiescence; checks receive evasions at the horizon. Scores combine conventional material values with our own geometric piece-square functions, rather than copied tables. Mate scores take priority. The displayed score terms describe the end of the searched line; they are not a causal explanation of chess strategy. Root candidates receive full-window searches at each completed depth. There is no opening book or rating claim.

Run `node projects/gambit/tests/run-all.js` from the portfolio root (or `node tests/run-all.js` here). It checks published perft counts for the starting position to depth 4, Kiwipete to 3, position 3 to 4, position 4 to 4 and position 5 to 3; SAN/FEN, promotions, pinned en passant, repetition, draws; ten mate-in-one/two positions re-verified with a separate bounded minimax solver; deterministic fixed-depth search; and legal replies across 1,000 seeded random-play positions. The generated KQK mate fixtures are deliberately small exhaustive puzzles, not a benchmark of tactical strength.

Serve the portfolio with `python -m http.server 8137`. `tests/browser.html` measures timer delay and main-thread long tasks during a worker search. `tests/browser-results.json` records the actual browser run. Preview positions in `preview.js` were generated by this engine at fixed depth 2 after the opening e4 e5 Nf3 Nc6 Bc4 Nf6. Playback is a lightweight twelve-position loop, with no search worker or runtime requests; it follows `Kit.onPlay` and reduced motion. The paused card shows a complete middle-game position.

SVG pieces are original simple silhouettes. Fonts are self-hosted Libre Baskerville and Inconsolata, under SIL OFL; pinned source metadata and licences are in `fonts/`. Standalone project repositories expect the shared `../kit.css` and `../kit.js` supplied by the portfolio; to preview one alone, place it under a `projects/` folder alongside those files.
