The extra rules
  • Captured pieces become yours.
  • Place a reserve piece on an empty square. That uses your turn.
  • Pawns can’t be placed on the first or last rank.
  • A promoted piece returns as a pawn when captured.
  • You can block a check or give checkmate with a drop.

Normal chess movement, castling and promotion apply. Stalemate and threefold repetition draw; there is no fifty-move draw.
