The agent’s world model

Six possible states, exactly one true: healthy, pool exhaustion, queue backlog, upstream timeout, schema mismatch or stale cache. A tool’s categorical signal eliminates incompatible states. These signals are noiseless and the list is exhaustive by construction.

The adaptive policy maximises expected candidate elimination per cost unit with equal prior weights. Its input is the observation history, never the hidden scenario. Logs and metrics cost 1 unit; targeted probes cost 2.

    What this experiment can establish

    Agency you can inspect

    This is a live deterministic diagnostic agent, not an LLM. It selects a tool from current evidence, receives an observation, revises the remaining hypotheses and stops at a single consistent state or its budget. There are no prerecorded diagnoses.

    A singleton is “identified within this model”, not proof about a real service. The quick policy guesses before it has unique evidence; that difference is visible in the conclusion.

    A small, closed world

    Only six fault types, no compound faults, no sensor noise and no remediation. A request-body instruction in the log is displayed as untrusted text and is never passed into policy logic. That separation is not a prompt-injection benchmark.
