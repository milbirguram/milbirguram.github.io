Echo

Citogenesis is when a claim goes onto Wikipedia unsourced, a writer repeats it, and that writing is then cited back as its source. Echo checks one sentence for the trace this leaves: a source dated after the sentence first appeared on Wikipedia. Open a case file, or load any article and pick a cited sentence. Dates show order, not copying, and Echo matches words, not meaning, so a flag is a reason to look closer, not proof. Article text is from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0.
