STABILITY CHECK

The evidence, assumptions and data

How the index works

Player profiles combine the 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26 seasons with weights 0.3, 0.6 and 1. Rates are pulled towards the forward or defence average when evidence is thin, then compared within that group.

Teams use their own 2025–26 team totals. A need is the amount a result falls below the league average, capped at three standard deviations. Team need and player strength are different quantities; an index point is not an expected goal.

At least 300 minutes at five-on-five in 2025–26 are required. Goalies and special teams are outside this model. A listed team is excluded by default.

What was actually checked

This tests next-season profile estimation, not whether a hypothetical move would succeed. The stability check omits one of a player’s seasons at a time while keeping team needs fixed; it is not a confidence interval.

On/off results still contain teammate, opponent and usage effects. Passing, line chemistry, injuries, contracts, cap space, trade cost and a coach’s intended role are not measured.
