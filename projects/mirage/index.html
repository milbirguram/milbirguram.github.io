Mirage

A made-up study asks whether a morning walk lifts mood, and you are the analyst. Make six ordinary analysis choices, each with a good reason, then see every path you could have taken and run a thousand more studies. The study is simulated and its six forks are a tidy stand-in for the messier choices real analyses face; the first study shown was picked so that a path through it works, as the page says at the end.

120 adults, split by lot: 60 walk every morning for two weeks, 60 carry on as usual. The data may or may not contain an effect. Six decisions; each option has a reason.
