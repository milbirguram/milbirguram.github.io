Stencil

A small U-Net outlines cell nuclei in microscope images it never saw in training, and every score is recomputed as you move the threshold. Press “Predict nothing” and pixel accuracy still reads 0.70–0.96, because most of each image is background, while Dice falls to zero.

Images: BBBC039v1 (Caicedo et al.), Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection, CC0.
